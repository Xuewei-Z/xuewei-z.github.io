01 — About

Hi, I'm Xuewei.

I started my career in credit insurance, backing loans made by banks and lending platforms. Their repayment data often showed what the lenders didn't say: a looser approval policy, a growing appetite for risk, or borrowers weaker than they first seemed.

Five years in, I was convinced that data tells the real story, so I pursued an M.S. in Statistics focused on data science and machine learning.

Statistics taught me to question a number before trusting it. As generative AI took off during my program, that rigor proved just as essential for AI, from scoring outputs with an LLM judge to A/B testing whether a change really helped.

Now at a healthcare AI startup, I build AI hands-on: an agent my colleagues use every day, language models I fine-tune and evaluate, and new projects with a small team I lead.

I'm still growing in both directions: building production AI systems and turning data into decisions through analytics and risk analysis. Each needs its own toolkit, but the discipline carries over: define the right metric, test the assumptions, and trust only what the evidence supports.
