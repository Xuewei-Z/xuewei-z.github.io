Hotel recommender system

Do review sentiment and timing make hotel recommendations better? I compared three model families on ~12M reviews to find out.

The problem

Most recommenders only learn from ratings. Reviews also carry sentiment, and they happen at a point in time. The question was whether those signals improve predictions.

What I did

  • Built and compared three approaches: SVD, neural collaborative filtering (NCF), and a hybrid model.
  • Engineered review-sentiment and time features.
  • Evaluated each user segment separately, instead of relying on a single average.

Result

Adding sentiment and time features cut RMSE by 11–15% compared with SVD, in all three user segments.

What didn't work

NCF on its own gave no gain over SVD. The improvement came from the features, not from a more complex model.
